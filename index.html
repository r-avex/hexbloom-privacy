Hexbloom Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

What the game stores

Hexbloom saves your scores, Journey progress, petals, themes, quests, settings, boosters and unfinished games on your device only. We do not run servers and we do not collect this data.

Advertising

Hexbloom shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers, approximate location (from the IP address), and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. On first launch you can choose your consent options (in regions where this is required) and whether to allow tracking (Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt). If you decline tracking, you still get ads, but they are not personalized. You can change ad privacy choices at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices, where shown, and tracking in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Children

Hexbloom is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.
