Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Write to quitboss45@gmail.com. Please include your device model and iOS version.

Common questions

How do I play? Open Settings > How to play in the game for the rules, tips and answers.

I lost my progress. Progress is saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes it.

I bought a purchase on another device. Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases with the same Apple ID.
